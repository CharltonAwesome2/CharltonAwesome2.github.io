## 4. General work patterns and prioritisation

Throughout the reporting period, I worked across multiple projects and adjusted priorities as requirements arose.

My general approach was to:
- Consider the importance and size of incoming requirements.
- Decide whether to finish the current task or complete an important milestone.
- Move to another project when its priority justified doing so.
- Investigate requirements before implementing changes.
- Test functionality and address issues as development progressed.

Although this approach was not managed through formal Scrum sprint planning, it allowed me to manage competing responsibilities and respond to changing requirements.

## 5. Challenges encountered

### Understanding requirements

Requirements were generally communicated by my supervisor. I often had to investigate how a system should behave and determine an appropriate technical implementation.

This required independent research, analysis and problem-solving.

### Reengineering existing work

As I gained more knowledge of the systems and explored alternative approaches, I sometimes identified shortcomings in previous implementations.

This required reviewing existing code, improving implementations and considering backward compatibility.

### Managing competing priorities

Working across multiple projects required decisions about which tasks to complete first. The importance and size of new requirements influenced whether I continued existing work or moved to another project.

### Maintaining documentation

I did not consistently maintain detailed daily development records. Reconstructing this logbook highlighted the difficulty of relying on Git history and recollection alone to establish a complete record of work.
