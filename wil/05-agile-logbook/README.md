# Agile Logbook

## Overview

This section records my professional development activities during the 2026 Work Integrated Learning period.

The logbook provides a retrospective account of the work performed across Projects A-D, including development activities, changing priorities, challenges and lessons learned.

## Logbook structure

The logbook uses chronological activity summaries rather than claiming to reproduce a complete daily timesheet.

Each entry identifies:
- The approximate period of activity.
- The project or projects involved.
- The general development activities undertaken.
- How work was prioritised.
- Relevant challenges or learning outcomes.

## Working hours

Working hours varied according to workload, project priorities and university commitments.

My approximate recollection is:

- **First semester:** Approximately four hours per working day, alongside university classes.
- **Second semester:** Approximately seven hours per day from Tuesday to Friday, as university classes were generally scheduled on Mondays.

University assignments also affected the time available for professional work, usually occupying approximately one day per week and occasionally more.

These figures are retrospective estimates, not exact recorded working hours.

## Retrospective approach

The logbook was reconstructed using available development history, project milestones, stakeholder communication and personal recollection.

Git commit dates provide evidence of recorded development activity, but they do not establish the exact hours worked or every activity performed on a particular day.

The entries therefore avoid inventing daily activities, precise working hours or meetings that were not recorded.

All project and stakeholder references have been anonymised for publication.
