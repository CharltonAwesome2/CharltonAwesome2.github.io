# Sprint Backlog

## 1. Purpose

This document presents a retrospective representation of development work across Projects A-D, organised into development iterations.

A sprint backlog normally contains the work selected for a sprint, together with a sprint goal and the tasks required to achieve it.

Formal Scrum sprints were not conducted during these projects. The iterations in this document are therefore reconstructed groupings of related development activities. They are intended to demonstrate how the work could be organised using sprint-backlog principles, rather than represent actual sprint plans or historical sprint commitments.

No specific sprint dates or durations are assigned because formal sprint timeboxes were not recorded.

## 6. Retrospective summary

The reconstructed iterations show how related requirements could be grouped into manageable development periods.

They also demonstrate that iteration outcomes can differ. Some groupings resulted in completed functionality, while others contained partially completed or outstanding work.

The groupings are based on related functionality and do not establish the exact historical order or duration of development activities.

## 7. Retrospective note

These iterations were prepared retrospectively for the purpose of demonstrating sprint-backlog concepts. They were not formal Scrum sprints, and no original sprint commitments, sprint-planning meetings or sprint-review records existed.

The iteration goals and selected backlog items represent a structured interpretation of the development work performed.
